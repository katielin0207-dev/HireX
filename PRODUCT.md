# Product

## Register

product

## Users

HR, recruiters, hiring managers and interviewers in enterprise recruitment workflows. They use the product during resume screening, risk review, interview preparation, interview evaluation and candidate decision meetings.

## Product Purpose

HireX is an AI recruiting selection agent layered above an existing ATS or internal recruitment system. It reduces repetitive manual review, standardizes candidate assessment, highlights hiring risks and turns interview notes into structured decision artifacts.

## Brand Personality

Professional, calm, efficient. The product should feel like a reliable HR workbench rather than a marketing page or generic AI chatbot.

## Anti-references

Avoid decorative SaaS landing-page layouts, oversized hero sections, vague AI summaries, long prose-heavy reports, and playful consumer-style visuals. The interface should not look like a replacement recruitment website; it should look like a decision-support tool for HR.

## Design Principles

- Decision first: show whether to proceed before showing evidence.
- Evidence over opinion: every risk needs a reason, source and next action.
- HR-friendly density: compact enough for repeated use, but not a wall of text.
- Workflow continuity: outputs should map to HR, hiring manager and interview follow-up actions.
- Demo resilience: key flows must work with mock data even when the LLM is slow.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use clear semantic colors with text labels, not color alone. Keep body text readable, support reduced cognitive load through progressive disclosure, and avoid motion that distracts from task completion.
